Most "sales problems" are actually memory problems.

The deal didn't die because the pitch was weak. It died in the 11 days between "send me a proposal" and someone remembering to follow up.

Pull your last 20 lost deals. Tag each: never qualified, lost on merit, or went dark after a specific step.

Most teams expect mostly "lost on merit." They find mostly "went dark."

You don't have a talent gap. You have one unowned handoff leaking pipeline, invisibly, every month.

Name the handoff. Name the owner. Set the clock.
